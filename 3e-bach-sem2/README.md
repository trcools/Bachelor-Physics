## 📘 Course Overviews

1. **Atoom- en Moleculaire Fysica (Atomic and Molecular Physics)**
   - Multi-electron atoms, central field approximation, LS and jj coupling schemes.
   - Diatomic and polyatomic molecules, Born-Oppenheimer approximation, rotational and vibrational spectra, electronic transitions and Franck-Condon principle.

2. **Elementary Particle Physics**
   - The Standard Model of particle physics: quarks, leptons, and gauge bosons.
   - Symmetries, conservation laws (isospin, strangeness, parity, CP violation).
   - Relativistic kinematics, Feynman diagrams, QED, QCD, and Electroweak interaction basics.

3. **Nuclear Physics**
   - Nuclear properties: mass, radius, binding energy (liquid drop model / Bethe-Weizsäcker formula).
   - Nuclear force and deuteron system, nuclear shell model and magic numbers.
   - Radioactivity: alpha, beta, and gamma decay, nuclear reactions and fission/fusion.

4. **Vaste Stof (Solid State Physics)**
   - Crystal structures, Bravais lattices, reciprocal lattice, X-ray diffraction (Bragg and Laue conditions).
   - Phonons and lattice vibrations, heat capacity (Einstein and Debye models).
   - Free electron Fermi gas, energy bands (Bloch theorem, Kronig-Penney model), metals, semiconductors, and insulators.
