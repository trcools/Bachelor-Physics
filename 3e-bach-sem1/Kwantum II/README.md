Lecture notes, theoretical proofs, problem sets, and past examination problems for **Kwantummechanica II** (3rd Bachelor Physics & Astronomy).

---

## 📘 Topics Covered

- **Perturbation Theory**: Time-independent perturbation theory (non-degenerate & degenerate cases), fine structure of hydrogen (relativistic correction, spin-orbit coupling, Darwin term), Zeeman effect and Stark effect.
- **Variational Principle & Approximation Methods**: Ground state upper bounds, trial wavefunctions, WKB approximation, connection formulas.
- **Time-Dependent Perturbation Theory**: Transition probabilities, Fermi's Golden Rule, harmonic perturbations, emission and absorption of radiation, selection rules.
- **Scattering Theory**: Partial wave analysis, phase shifts, optical theorem, Born approximation and Lippmann-Schwinger equation.
- **Identical Particles & Many-Body Systems**: Fermions and bosons, Slater determinants, exchange interaction.
